## Torrent recovery

- Turn on recovery in **Settings → Torrents** to search Internet Archive after an eligible direct download runs out of retries. Choose to review each match or continue automatically when a single candidate passes verification and can reuse at least two saved pieces.
- Check a local torrent file, a magnet link, or a public HTTP(S) torrent link from the download window. Auralis keeps the original partial file, reuses verified pieces, and downloads the remaining pieces via BitTorrent. The download list marks the transfer **Direct → Torrent**.

## Download backup

- **Settings → General → Download backup** creates a portable archive with preferences, history, queues, completed files, and supported paused transfers. It supports direct downloads, public torrents, and tracked gallery and video downloads, including saved subtitles.
- Restore places archived data in a new folder and preserves existing files. The backup dialog identifies transfers that require credentials or have data it cannot safely archive.
