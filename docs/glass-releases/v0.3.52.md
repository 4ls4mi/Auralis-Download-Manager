## Appearance refinements

- Keeps the Sculpted Liquid Glass animation inside the Bubble progress shape and makes the unfilled glass more translucent.
- Reduces stacked surface opacity in desktop-glass main windows so those layers cover less of the backdrop.
- Refines Aurora Glass surfaces and restores distinct action-button colors and highlights.

The material appearance and native acrylic depend on Windows and still require visual verification on the user's desktop. The irregular floating Frosted Layers outline does not use native acrylic because of its clipping limitation. Full backup and restore for torrents and other multi-file downloads remains in development.
