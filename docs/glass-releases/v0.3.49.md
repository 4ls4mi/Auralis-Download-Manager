## Torrent recovery

- Choose whether to search manually, search automatically and review results, or allow automatic continuation when one candidate matches at least two saved pieces and all returned candidates were checked. Automatic search is off by default and is limited to eligible failed, non-resumable ordinary downloads after queue retries are exhausted.
- Searching sends filename terms to Internet Archive. Continuing uses BitTorrent peers. A match verifies only the pieces compared; it does not prove the unseen remainder is the intended file. The original partial download is kept, and recovery needs additional disk space. Private downloads and files with an expected whole-file checksum are excluded from automatic recovery.
- The download window shows recovery search and handoff status, allows manual torrent or magnet selection, and identifies a direct download continued as a torrent. Cancelling a pending automatic search prevents its later handoff.

## Appearance and browser extension

- Adds Black Blur Glass, a translucent dark surface that requests Windows acrylic when desktop transparency is selected and supported.
- Modernizes the cookie-source menu in the video panels, removes the unavailable subtitle burn-in option, and shows the Auralis version reported by the browser helper. The extension packages are version 0.1.15; Chrome and Edge store updates require their own submission.
- Names the installed browser helper `auralis-browser-host.exe` while retaining compatibility with older installations.

Full backup and restore for torrents and other multi-file downloads is still in development. Windows visual results depend on the system's backdrop support and settings.
