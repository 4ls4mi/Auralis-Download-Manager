## Transfers

- Connection blocks in full and compact views use the available pane width to pack into horizontal rows, with a shared flow rail above each row.

## Appearance

- Progress fill settings and independent track finish and color controls are under Appearance → Indicators. The track supports suggested colors, palettes, and the graphical color picker. Classic materials can opt in to the custom fill.
- Restore the classic customization hint from View → Customization hint.
