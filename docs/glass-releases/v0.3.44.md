## Update notices and default appearance

- The Windows-notification setting now also opens an Auralis corner popup with a button to review and download the update. The popup is placed above the usual Windows notification area and opens without taking keyboard focus. An Auralis-only corner-popup option is also available; the in-app notice remains in every mode.
- The corner popup is suppressed while Windows reports presentation, full-screen or quiet states. The in-app notice remains available.
- New installations use the Glacier Blue theme, Rose Pine surface, Aurora indicators, and Soft Aero Water progress and connection fills. Existing saved appearance choices are retained.
