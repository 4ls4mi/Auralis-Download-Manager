## Fractured Acrylic across Auralis

The main window now has center cuts in its title bar, menu strip, and footer. Settings, download progress, Start Download, Completed Download, and the other Auralis dialogs use the same separated glass surfaces. Space between the panels shows the desktop or window behind Auralis.

Open **Settings → Appearance → Materials and color → Panel finish** while using Fractured Acrylic to choose Acrylic, Mica, Tinted, Blurred, Translucent, or Frosted Glass. Your selection is saved. Native Acrylic and Mica effects depend on Windows support.
