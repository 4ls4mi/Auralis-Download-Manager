## Materials and transfer visuals

- Carbon is the new default for fresh profiles. Prism Glass has brighter translucent layers, and Porcelain adds a light opaque material. Existing profiles keep their selected material. An optional accent override lets you apply a custom highlight color across materials.
- Toolbar and caption controls have brighter action colors and clearer hover and press states. The minimize glyph is visible on the main window.
- Bubble is a progress shape with its own liquid animation. It works with every progress palette and fill setting. Connection cells fill from the bottom, water flows through the active transfer tubes, and graph and connection colors can be selected independently.

## Windows, downloads, and settings

- Main-window minimize and close-to-tray settings are back. Transfer windows can minimize to the tray independently, and hidden transfers can be reopened from the tray.
- Start Download and confirmations open in separate themed windows. The download list restores selection checkboxes and bulk removal, and puts status and speed below each filename alongside progress, connections, and activity.
- Dropdowns show their current selection. Browse for a video-helper browser profile or choose a detected one. Notification settings offer in-app, Windows, corner, and release-details modes, plus event-specific built-in or custom WAV sounds with preview.
