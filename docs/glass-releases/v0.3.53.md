## Glass appearance

- The Bubble progress bar blends into the window outside its outline. Its glass has iridescent reflections and keeps the movement of Sculpted Liquid Glass.
- Connections and transfer graphs use a palette-colored glow with every fill style and graph shape.
- Desktop acrylic and the visible window frame use matching bounds and corner radii.
- Aurora Glass uses separate translucent panes, with rounded reflections in Dark and ice facets in Light. The action buttons have beveled faces and distinct colors.

Native acrylic depends on Windows backdrop support and the selected background mode.
