## Black Ceramic and Monochrome Studio

- Black Ceramic is an opaque charcoal material with warm coral highlights and orange neon liquid. Monochrome Studio is an opaque white material with violet highlights, mint neon liquid, and labels for existing keyboard shortcuts.
- Choose either material in Settings under Appearance. The Use material colors option follows the selected material. Explicit progress, connection, and graph palettes are saved independently.

## Windows and transfer controls

- Resize the main window, Settings, transfer details, and detached dialogs from their edges or corners. Detached dialogs keep the size you set when they reopen.
- All progress shapes animate neon-lit liquid in both fill styles. Active tube outlets release droplets into their connection cells.
- Indicator settings show visible labels and display their saved selections on open.
- Menus use readable themed text, semantic icons, and animated hover and keyboard highlights. The download context menu supports Shift+F10.
