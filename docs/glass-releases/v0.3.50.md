## Appearance and controls

- Adds the Aurora Glass dark and light materials, with layered surfaces and vivid action buttons.
- Adds compact themed scrollbars and refreshes the progress, connection and graph indicators with luminous gradients. The indicator fill choices are Default and Sculpted Liquid Glass; removed legacy choices are mapped to supported options.
- Adds a Bubble shape and updates action-button styling in Frosted Layers, Smoked Glass and Pearl Frost.
- Updates title-bar double-click handling and retains theme import/export, including the new materials and Bubble shape.

Windows visual appearance and native acrylic depend on Windows support and background settings. The irregular floating Frosted Layers outline does not currently use native acrylic because the native backdrop can paint a rectangular area around the shape.

Full backup and restore for torrents and other multi-file downloads remains in development.
