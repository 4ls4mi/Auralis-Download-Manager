## Glass appearance

- Auralis windows now have square edges that match the Windows backdrop.
- Aurora Glass Dark and Light use desktop acrylic in the main window and Settings. Their tinted glass layers and edge lighting let the desktop remain visible.
- Use the **Glass translucency** slider under **Settings → Appearance** to adjust the glass from 0% to 100%. Choose **Use material default** to restore the preset. Theme exports and preference backups carry your setting.
- The built-in materials have updated panel depth, shading, and highlights. Action buttons have thinner translucent faces and clearer hover, keyboard focus, and disabled states.

Windows controls desktop acrylic availability through its transparency settings and backdrop support.
